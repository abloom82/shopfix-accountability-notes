# Accountability Notes (ShopFix Tribe)

A shareable copy of the **Sticky Notes** on PNW's Front Counter Board.
Pick one goal, pick a buddy, send them the link.

- **You:** write the goal, choose how often to check in (every day,
  weekdays, once a week, or just once), and send the link by text or email.
  Your notes stay on your phone/computer under "My notes", where you log
  each win and can text your buddy an update.
- **Your buddy:** taps the link, adds repeating reminders to Google
  Calendar or iPhone/Outlook, and taps "Text" to send you a nudge that's
  already written.

Your note glows magenta and your buddy's glows baby blue, same as the
shop board. Keep it positive: it counts wins, never misses.

## How it works (plain English)

It's one file, `index.html`, with no database and no sign-ups. The goal
is packed into the link after the `#`, so whoever has the link sees
the note. The part after `#` is never sent to the web server.

## Versions
